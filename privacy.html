PRIVACY POLICY

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 14, 2026  ·  Entity: HarmaalWale, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India

HarmaalWale ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using harmaalwale.com (the "Site") you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account details — name, mobile number, email address, password, date of birth, gender
  • Address — delivery addresses including pincode and landmark
  • Order information — items purchased, quantity, payment method, billing address
  • Communications — messages sent through our support form, WhatsApp, or email
  • Vendor information — business name, GST/PAN, bank details (for approved sellers only)

1.2 Information we collect automatically

  • Device information (browser type, IP address, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages visited, products viewed, time spent)
  • Cookies and local storage tokens to keep you logged in

1.3 Information from payment partners

When you pay via Razorpay or PayU, we receive a payment confirmation and transaction ID. We do NOT see or store your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process orders, payments, and deliveries
  • Send OTPs for mobile verification
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support replies
  • Improve our products, website, and customer experience
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized use
  • Comply with applicable Indian laws (GST, consumer protection, etc.)

3. How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Logistics partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, etc.) — for delivery only
  • Payment gateways (Razorpay, PayU) — for processing payments
  • SMS provider (Fast2SMS) — to send OTPs and order updates
  • Email service — for transactional emails
  • Government authorities — when legally required

4. Data Security

We protect your data using industry-standard measures:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • OTP-based mobile verification
  • JWT-style auth tokens with 30-day expiry
  • Access controls on admin and vendor panels

While we follow best practices, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You're responsible for keeping your password confidential.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use browser local storage to remember your login session, cart items, wishlist, and theme preference. We do NOT use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — view it in your Account dashboard
  • Update your information — through "Edit Profile" in your account
  • Delete your account — email support@harmaalwale.com
  • Withdraw consent — at any time by closing your account
  • Export your data — request a copy by emailing support

7. Children's Privacy

HarmaalWale is intended for users 18+ years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have, we will delete the data immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain:

  • Account data — as long as your account is active
  • Order records — for 7 years (Indian tax law requirement)
  • Support tickets — for 2 years
  • OTP codes — deleted after 10 minutes

9. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party services (WhatsApp, payment partners). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies separately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via email or a prominent notice on the Site. Your continued use of the Site after the changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us
