ABOUT HARMAALWALE

Your everyday marketplace — built in Jaipur, trusted across India

Our Story

HarmaalWale started in 2024 in the bylanes of Jaipur with a simple belief: every customer in India deserves easy access to genuine products and honest service — whether it's a hard-to-find printer spare or a piece of Jaipuri fashion.

What began as a small office sourcing spare parts for local copier shops has grown into a multi-category marketplace. We now ship MFD spares, refurbished machines, electronics, and fashion across all 28 states of India.

Our name "HarmaalWale" comes from the Hindi word "har maal" — meaning "everything you need". And that's exactly what we aim to deliver — every product, every time.
