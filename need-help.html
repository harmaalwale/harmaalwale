Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order? +
Browse products → Add to Cart → Click Cart icon → Proceed to Checkout → Select address → Choose payment method → Confirm order.
How long does delivery take? +
Typically 3–7 business days depending on your location. We deliver pan India with tracked shipments.
What payment methods do you accept? +
Razorpay (UPI, Cards, Net Banking, Wallets), PayU, and Cash on Delivery (select locations).
Can I return or replace a product? +
Yes — within 48 hours of delivery for damaged or wrong items. Contact us via WhatsApp or the support form with photos.
Are MFD spare parts genuine? +
We stock OEM-genuine and high-quality compatible parts. Each listing mentions origin (Japan, China, Thailand) and part number.
Do refurbished MFDs have warranty? +
Yes, all refurbished machines come with warranty. Duration varies by model — contact us for specifics.
How do I become a seller? +
Visit the Sell on HarmaalWale page, fill the application form, we'll review within 48 hours.
Can I cancel my order? +
Yes, orders can be cancelled before shipment. Go to My Account → Orders → Cancel, or WhatsApp us with your order ID.
How do I create an account? +
Click the account icon at top right → Enter your mobile → Verify with OTP → Done! Email/password also supported.
Forgot password — what do I do? +
Use mobile OTP login to access your account, then change password from Edit Profile. Or contact us for help.

Still need help?

Our support team is here 24/7. Reach out via WhatsApp, email, or send a detailed message.
